# Strategic Frameworks

Theoretical and practical frameworks supporting the three flagship projects.

## Contents

- **La Distincion Framework** - Decision-making and differentiation models
- **Marketing Frameworks** - Strategic positioning and value communication
- **DoD Framework 5000.2** - Systems engineering and project management
- **Zachman Framework** - Enterprise architecture and information systems

## Application

These frameworks provide the theoretical foundation for:
- **Ambient-Gantt**: Agency detection and behavioral modeling
- **Account Ninja**: Financial decision-making and priority frameworks  
- **Integrated Analytics**: Systems architecture and analytical methodologies

## Integration Points

Each framework contributes specific methodologies that enhance project capabilities and provide structured approaches to complex problem-solving across enterprise, behavioral, and analytical domains.
